# Seven recommended PathwayAI visuals

1. Forest sunlight banner: the existing landing-page photo. Keep it short and below the pathway choices.
2. Woodland walking trail: Learn & Prevent; suitable for outdoor planning.
3. Forest canopy: an optional alternate nature banner. It is not a tick photograph, despite its original internal key tick_leaf.
4. Peaceful lake: a calm alternate Care & Support banner; avoid using all four landscape photos on the same page.
5. Care cost journey: Care & Support explanation of possible care steps and expense categories. Conceptual illustration, not a required clinical pathway or study result.
6. Hidden cost categories: community burden explanation. Conceptual categories, not numerical study findings. The Hook reference does not mean this entire graphic is a reproduced study figure or establishes a caregiver cost estimate.
7. Fictional money-and-time card: product demonstration with $180 medical payments, $45 other payments, 3 workdays and 8 caregiver hours. Synthetic example, not a real patient record. SVG opens in a browser and scales without loss.

Recommended core set: 1, 2, 5, 6 and 7. Images 3 and 4 are alternatives, rather than reasons to add more decoration. Use one main visual per section; keep captions as selectable text in the app. The two infographics contain small text, so keep the accessible text explanations alongside them.

These files were extracted unchanged from existing PathwayAI assets, except the SVG generated by the existing app renderer using clearly labeled fictional data. No new stock photographs were acquired. This package does not grant a new license or independently establish the original photographic rights. It excludes the removed AI attached-tick illustration and the external photos discussed in chat.
