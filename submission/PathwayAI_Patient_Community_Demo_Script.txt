As a pathologist, I studied disease through a microscope. As an epidemiologist, I studied patterns across communities.

Then it became personal.

After I lost my job, my family lost our health insurance. The next month, my husband was bitten by a tick and became ill with Lyme disease.

For immigrants like my husband, language can add another barrier: understanding tests, asking about bills, and explaining symptoms.

I built PathwayAI to help people prepare for care and make these hidden burdens visible.

I'm Yuwei Zhang. Let me show you.

PathwayAI follows the whole journey. Before a bite, people can prepare for time outdoors. After a bite, they can find removal steps and time-sensitive care guidance.
