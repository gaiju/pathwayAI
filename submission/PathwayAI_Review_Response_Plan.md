## Final demonstration emphasis

The video keeps the approved personal introduction and uses the official listed project title, PathwayAI: Full Economic Burden, with Making the Hidden Patient Journey Visible as subtitle, names the Cost of Illness challenge, briefly covers the whole pathway, and shows the personal cost/time record before community planning. It explains affordable-care navigation because the introduction raises insurance loss. It now gives a concrete transport example. Prevention, coordinated records and earlier access support the ambition of reducing burden; clinical and economic benefits have not been established for this app.

The original mockup's simulated savings, matched-cohort labels and Bayesian trajectories are not current results. The separate Q&A explains why: public CMS aggregate files do not reconstruct beneficiary-level pre-diagnosis journeys, and a valid longitudinal/causal model requires appropriate access, clinical definitions, methods and validation.
