# PathwayAI main downloads

## Patient-facing: 12 output types

1. Outdoor preparation plan (new).
2. Reviewed visit summary.
3. Appointment and cost summary.
4. Money-and-time image (SVG).
5. Support request.
6. Support summary, including optional bill details.
7. Benefits preparation checklist.
8. Uninsured-care and medical-bill questions.
9. Lyme testing questions.
10. Original notes.
11. Original and reviewed English summary (conditional on translation).
12. Interpreter request.

This counts output types, not files required per patient. Detailed-mode versions of visit/cost summaries are variants, not additional report types. Most users only need selected outputs. Translation outputs require permission and available AI. Outdoor downloads contain general guidance and destination/month/activity, not maps or selected health conditions.

All written report downloads use PDF and carry: “PathwayAI: Making the Hidden Patient Journey Visible—and Actionable.” The money-and-time image carries the same visible tagline. QR downloads now include it beneath the original, unchanged QR bitmap. CSV downloads carry it in a new `PathwayAI report` metadata column; original data columns and values are retained. Original-notes PDFs have a branded header while preserving the note body.


## Separate community/research outputs

Two main community brief types: a community planning brief and a patient voice-to-action brief. Optional AI evidence notes and data/scenario CSVs are additional research exports; availability depends on the chosen tool and loaded data. They are not patient clinical reports and are not included in the 12 above.

## Shared entry across patient pages

Tick Bite: What Next? and Care & Support reuse the story organizer within the same app session. Patients do not need to enter the same story twice. The bite page focuses on immediate information; Care & Support adds money/time records and support resources. Guidance can be read without filling out a story.

## Where to find the downloads

| App page | Main downloads |
|---|---|
| Learn & Prevent | Outdoor preparation plan PDF with destination state outline when available |
| Tick Bite: What Next? | Visit summary; original notes; bilingual summary and interpreter request when available; Lyme testing questions |
| Care & Support | Same shared visit/language outputs; appointment and cost summary; money-and-time SVG; support request and summary; benefits checklist; uninsured-care and bill questions; detailed-mode summary variants |
| Community Burden & Action | Planning brief; patient voice-to-action brief; county rates PDFs by highest rate or A–Z; rate CSVs in both orders; historical rate CSV; Medicare comparison CSV; burden comparisons, scenarios and assumptions; other evidence/context exports when the corresponding sections are available |
| Start page / share panels | Branded QR image; report downloads are reached through the relevant section |
| Share Feedback / What’s next | No personal report required; share panels may offer the QR image |
| Sources & Acknowledgements | Source links; no separate main patient report |

Downloads depend on the selected mode, available data, entered information and AI consent/configuration. A file type is not an additional form the patient must complete.
