# Patient report review: implementation and verification boundaries

## Implemented

1. **Visit preparation:** the same organizer is available after urgent instructions on Tick Bite and in Care & Support. English summaries now begin with an exposure-to-care timeline: exposure, tick observation, removal timing, onset, rash, symptoms/duration, tests/results, diagnosis timing and care history. Local extraction preserves original sentences rather than silently converting a positive/negative result to a generic testing flag. Missing bite/rash information stays missing. Users can edit each field and the final document; original notes remain at the end. Source excerpts may appear in more than one relevant field. They are not clinical conclusions or a complete linguistic parser, and ambiguous time periods require review.

2. **Optional bilingual notes:** patients select a writing language and enter notes in their preferred language. External AI is called only after permission and an explicit translation click, using the existing API configuration and quota controls. The prompt asks for faithful English text, an exposure-to-care chronology and clarification questions; it prohibits diagnosis, risk scoring, treatment advice and invented dates. Originals remain available, including after errors. Original and editable English output are shown together and can be downloaded. The original notes and a qualified-interpreter request can be downloaded without a successful translation. Both pages reuse the same source/result; changed notes, language or reporter invalidate the old result. This translates the entered notes, not the interface, safety guidance or every other report. AI-derived English details do not automatically populate clinical/cost fields. Full app localization, photos, HealthKit and certified interpretation are not implemented.

3. **Payment-help summary:** optional details for one bill include institution, approximate bill date, total, amount already paid, stated unpaid balance and financial-assistance status. These are not summed into personal spending totals. Relevant facts remain opt-in before inclusion; no account-number/document upload is requested. Multiple-bill history or verified account balances are not established.

4. **Benefits preparation:** short-term missed-work days and caregiver hours are no longer automatically inserted into SSI/SSDI preparation as disability evidence. The adult disability-based duration/other requirements are explained and linked to official program information; an optional functional timeline records activities, changes, dates and help needed. SSI aged/blind routes and distinct SNAP rules are noted. These remain preparation aids, not eligibility assessment or application services. Short-term observations can be useful personal context, but cannot establish the duration standard.

5. **County estimates:** the main county illustration remains Hook's societal scenario in 2016 dollars. In the calculator, the different medical-study reference is only available after explicit opt-in; it carries a visible warning about different study populations/dollar years and pending original-table verification. Its patient-payment references appear only with that medical scenario. The separate published medical table is collapsed, including in More detail mode. The default combined county brief also omits the medical-only county multiplication unless that benchmark was explicitly enabled. Published population benchmarks have been removed from the personal appointment/cost export, which now focuses on the patient's own entries. No inflation, underreporting, local case-mix or savings adjustment is invented.

6. **Community brief:** remains a human-reviewed planning template with sourced county context, a separate fictional demonstration, possible actions, responsibility/funding questions and measures. It is not a delivered patient service, observed needs assessment, funded partnership or measured benefit. The patient-facing outputs above are distinct from this indirect planning use. We have not replaced templates with a representative real county dataset; shared collection stays off.

## Translation and data handling

The configured external model receives only notes, the selected language and reporter role after consent. Existing API keys are reused without copying them into files. Quota/rate controls reserve each request. Model storage is requested off, which is not a guarantee of zero provider retention. No new shared patient file, automatic contribution, identity-document collection or health-system integration is added.

Validation checks structure, not medical/linguistic truth. Even well-formed output can mistranslate negation, dates, test names or medications. Patients and clinicians should use a qualified interpreter when needed. The software integration is tested using simulated responses and service failures; no live patient translation or clinical accuracy validation was performed during this update. Original notes must remain usable if the API is missing, unavailable, limited or returns malformed output.

## What this does not demonstrate

Earlier diagnosis, avoided disseminated disease, lower bills, disability prevention, successful benefit applications, improved access or causal cost savings have not been established. The Hook localized/disseminated difference remains an observational study-group comparison. Better report structure and technical tests are not evidence of those outcomes. A patient/clinician usability review and consented pilot are still needed.

## Verification record

- Full automated suite: 64 tests passed in 36.955 seconds after the report/export changes. Subsequent targeted reruns passed: translation/summary 5, benefits 5, bill-summary/persistence 2, and care access/navigation 3.
- Browser checks with synthetic notes: Chinese entry and selected language persisted from Tick Bite to Care & Support; the translation button was disabled before consent; local English summary retained a reported positive blood test, relative symptom-onset timing and diagnosis timing ahead of original notes; no page exceptions or horizontal overflow at a 390-pixel viewport.
- Additional Streamlit interaction check: the county calculator defaults to the societal reference; the medical benchmark needs explicit opt-in, displays its warning, and returns to the societal option when turned off without an exception.
- External translation request/response and failures were tested with simulated responses. No paid API requests or live patient translations were made. Clinical accuracy, outcomes and current external-service availability remain unverified.
