# Remaining questions for CMS and Sprint data stewards

Subject: Remaining CMS questions for PathwayAI — beneficiary access and cost measurement

Hi [Organizer name],

Thank you for offering to collect remaining questions. PathwayAI helps patients prepare for care and record costs, missed work and unpaid caregiving. We are exploring beneficiary-authorized access for a future phase; Blue Button is not currently connected.

Would you please forward these questions to CMS?

1. **Available records:** What coverage, historical period and update delay does Blue Button 2.0 currently support for inpatient, hospital outpatient, professional and prescription claims? How do Medicare Advantage and Part D coverage affect what is available? Does it include actual laboratory results or mainly billed services and diagnosis codes?

2. **Payment meaning:** Which fields should we use to keep Medicare payments, beneficiary cost-sharing responsibility and other-payer payments separate? What cannot be inferred about amounts the patient actually paid, an unpaid balance, or a final bill?

3. **Consent and future research:** What production onboarding, beneficiary authorization, privacy, security and retention requirements apply to an app supporting individual visit/cost summaries? What additional pathway and review would be needed to combine separately consented records across participants for longitudinal or county research? Please distinguish Blue Button developer terms from any research-data access or agreement requirements.

4. **Prediagnosis burden:** For research on services and payments before the first recorded Lyme diagnosis, what CMS data-access route would you recommend? Which limitations apply to treating the first claim code as diagnosis timing, and to county-level release with small numbers? We understand claims will not capture the full uninsured, unpaid caregiving or work-loss burden.

5. **Current county comparison:** We now use a supplied extract of the 2014–2024 Original Medicare Geographic Variation public use file. We compare Dutchess and Orange by beneficiary residence, using the All age group and separate actual/standardized inpatient and hospital outpatient per-capita payments. Are there interpretation or comparability cautions beyond age/health-status mix, payment-rate standardization, Original Medicare selection, suppression, and hospital outpatient not representing all ambulatory care?

If possible, we also have two questions for the relevant data stewards:

- **CDC/NYSDOH:** Which county Lyme surveillance series and methods should be used for comparisons across the 2022 reporting/definition changes? What wording best avoids interpreting changes in reporting as changes in personal risk or true incidence?
- **Census/ACS:** Which county tables and denominators best describe limited English proficiency or English-speaking ability, alongside language spoken at home? We do not want to treat speaking another language at home as proof of difficulty with English.

Written guidance or links to the appropriate documentation would be very helpful. We are not requesting identifiable beneficiary records by email.
