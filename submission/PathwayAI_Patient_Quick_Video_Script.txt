Need help getting ready for a visit?

Start with Quick and easy. Choose Prepare for care.

You can find care and bill resources without writing a story.

To make a visit note, choose your language. Write where and when it happened, your symptoms, and any tests or treatment. A few words are enough. Leave out your name. Skip anything you do not know.

For English notes, select Organize My Story. Check the summary, fix mistakes, and download it.

For another language, read the permission notice before using AI. Check the English beside your original words.

Costs and time are optional. Enter only what you know.

Bring your summary and original test reports to your clinician. PathwayAI helps you prepare. It does not diagnose.
