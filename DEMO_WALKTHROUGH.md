# Presentation walkthrough

## Opening

"PathwayAI helps make the invisible burden of Lyme disease visible: money paid
for care, missed work, unpaid caregiving and obstacles to support. The MVP helps
people prepare for care and shows how those experiences could inform community
priorities. Secure shared reporting is the next pilot phase."

## A short demonstration

1. Start in **Short and simple**. Explain that readers can change the amount of
   detail at any time without changing their entries.
2. Open **I need care & support**. Use the app's fictional story example or leave
   the story blank. External AI can remain off.
3. For a clearly labeled spoken fictional example, choose past 30 days and before
   diagnosis. Enter care spending $400, other spending $100, 3 missed workdays,
   6 caregiver hours and an assumed $200 per missed workday.
4. Review the card. Patient-paid spending is $500. The $600 work-loss scenario is
   separate; caregiver time stays 6 hours. Download the reviewed summary. These
   inputs demonstrate the workflow; they are not a real participant result.
5. Open the Lyme tests section. Show the preparation checklist, questions for a
   written price estimate and the distinction between individual antibody rows
   and the lab's overall result. Do not display a private patient report.
6. Open **Community Burden & Action**. Start with the New York map, Dutchess/Orange comparison and language context, then the improvement options and cost breakdown.
7. The private session preview reuses the entries made in Care & Support. Return to that page to show that costs, time, the story and reviewed edits are retained. It is not a shared county summary or automatic contribution.
8. Explain that county evidence requires separately reviewed consent and comparable contributions. Real secure shared reporting is the next pilot phase, not an operating live backend.
