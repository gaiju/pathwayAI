PATHWAYAI — COMPARISON FOR EXTERNAL REVIEW

Basis: five competitor descriptions supplied by the project lead. These are claimed capabilities, not independently verified product tests, clinical validation, rankings or judging results.

1. ImpactAtlas — Pranisha Karki's team
Focus: voice/text patient journeys, 15 burden dimensions, multiple conditions, reports, geographic comparisons and modeled scenarios; reports Spanish interaction.
Overlap: patient stories, household costs, federal data, multilingual support and community ambitions.
Lesson: make the patient output and missing-data labels easy to understand.

2. Pricing the Invisible, Valuing the Cure — Manuel Duenas
Focus: national policy valuation, initially Long COVID; treatment reach/effectiveness scenarios, quality-of-life valuation, source registers, sensitivity analysis and community-reported experience. Describes expansion to Lyme and other conditions.
Overlap: public evidence and burden beyond medical bills.
Lesson: show the source, population, price year and assumptions behind each number. A national treatment-value model is a different task from county support planning.

3. ImpactFile — Greg Michaelson
Focus: a longitudinal record reused for medical leave, disability, insurance appeals and tax documentation; describes on-device AI and encrypted iPhone storage.
Overlap: dated functional-impact records and documentation for support conversations.
Lesson: show how a downloaded record helps its next recipient. Do not imply PathwayAI's preparation record is a certified or approved benefits application.

4. DBbun — Uri Kartoun
Focus: study-based Lyme/Long COVID what-if simulators, observed/assumed/projected labels, federal context and transparent inflation comparisons.
Overlap: published cost references, scenario assumptions and source-linked calculations.
Lesson: retain original study-year dollars alongside optional CPI comparisons; do not convert selected-study findings into verified county totals or invent uncertainty distributions.

5. Health Worth — Trisha Rawlings
Focus: condition-based care journeys, episode pricing, insurance context and cost-of-delay scenarios; explicitly includes Lyme disease among its conditions.
Overlap: care preparation, financial barriers and journeys.
Lesson: make the user's next step clear and keep scenarios separate from individual predictions.

PATHWAYAI POSITIONING

PathwayAI combines practical Lyme prevention, care preparation, mixed-language records, household burden documentation and county public-evidence planning. The useful distinction is its focused workflow connecting what families need to what community teams should investigate, starting with Dutchess and Orange. County/community interest is not exclusive to PathwayAI.

CURRENT VERSUS PROPOSED

- Current main: existing patient tools, county comparisons, original-dollar cost illustrations and optional inflation comparison. A GitHub source snapshot is not independent confirmation of the live Streamlit deployment.
- Proposed community update: four community action areas, three audience choices, enhanced county brief with existing HRSA fields and explicitly limited survey context. Unmerged and not deployed.
- Patient records do not automatically become community evidence. Consent-based shared reporting is a future development proposal.
- Top-ten New York expansion is not complete or deployed. Verified counts for additional counties are still required before economic totals can be calculated.
- No demonstrated reductions in costs, delays or disability are claimed. No adopted county partnership or program is implied.

QUESTIONS FOR THE REVIEWER

1. Are public surveillance, research estimates, survey context and suggested actions clearly separated?
2. Are cost perspectives, reporting periods, dollar years and overlapping components handled correctly?
3. Is the patient download useful for a visit or support conversation?
4. Could a community team use the county brief to identify one practical question and the information needed next?
5. What one improvement should be prioritized before adding more features?
