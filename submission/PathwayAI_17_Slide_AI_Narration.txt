Our purpose is simple: make the hidden patient journey visible—and actionable.

The pathway starts before illness. Choose where and when you will be outdoors, and what you plan to do. Prevention belongs here.

Prepare suitable clothing and an EPA-registered repellent, following product instructions. After outdoor activities, check your body, clothes, gear, and pets. Shower within two hours. These precautions support risk reduction; they do not guarantee protection.

If a tick is attached, use fine-tipped tweezers, grasp close to the skin, and pull upward steadily. Record when and where it happened. Seek medical advice promptly when appropriate, including after some high-risk bites. Do not wait for symptoms if you need prevention advice.

Keep the date and possible exposure location in your record. That place may differ from where you live or need care.

Now, a fictional person feels tired after an outdoor walk and wants help preparing for care. They enter a short story, including payments, missed work, and unpaid help. PathwayAI organizes the details for them to check, change, and download.

The testing guide helps people prepare questions about symptoms, timing, the complete report, and costs. It supports a conversation with a clinician; it does not diagnose.

Some people need help reporting. Caregiver observations, the person’s daily-life difficulties, and the caregiver’s own experience can be recorded separately. Partial answers are welcome.

Here, the fictional person paid one hundred eighty dollars for care and forty-five dollars for travel: two hundred twenty-five dollars altogether. Three missed workdays and eight hours of unpaid help stay separate. Unknown answers stay unknown.

A record becomes more useful when it supports a next step. Here, someone can prepare a request for transport help, asking about eligibility, cost, booking, and a return trip.

Benefits preparation helps people organize questions and relevant records for food assistance or disability support. The agency determines eligibility. Nothing is submitted automatically.
