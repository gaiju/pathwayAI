Use PathwayAI_Patient_First_ElevenLabs_Script.txt for tagged narration, or the plain TXT for a voice model that does not support tags. No spoken stage/page labels are embedded in the plain script.

The community images return to action after the county context. If cards make the video too long, omit them; every content image already has a section title.

The fictional example is $180 care + $45 travel = $225 paid, with three workdays and eight caregiving hours separate. The live demonstration is local English organization with external AI off. Optional AI is described, not presented as a live response.

The script includes entrant-confirmed formative working-app review by Lyme patients and a county commissioner. It makes no claim of county adoption, clinically validated outcomes or proven savings. Usefulness, partnerships and sustainability remain proposed next steps.
