# AI voice and video instructions

The clean TXT/PDF is narration only. The voice directions file is for the tool's style/instructions field. Never paste production directions into a plain text-to-speech field. SSML works only in tools that explicitly support it. An unsupported tool may read tags aloud: use the clean script and insert silence in the video editor instead.

## What is ready

- A clean, short-paragraph English narration with the present-day patient/caregiver role and future county partnerships made explicit.
- Three black title cards, white text and three-second timings.
- Three-part structure: Problem → Understanding the Hidden Burden → From Information to Action.
- Large headlines, now including simple guiding questions; chapter/app-page labels at the bottom; primary highlights larger than the UI screenshots.
- A separate expressive-voice direction sheet and optional SSML with three chapter pauses, a 0.9-second pause before the visit example and a 0.8-second pause before the county-pilot example.

No audio has been rendered. Expressiveness, pronunciation, natural pauses and final length must be checked in the selected voice. The SSML's marked pauses total 10.7 seconds, including nine seconds for title cards; punctuation/engine pauses are additional. The 4:45 slide plan is a draft timing guide, not verified audio length. Adjust screen durations to the recorded voice; keep the final export below 5:00. Do not count the same SSML pause and editor silence twice.

## Editing steps

1. Add the presenter's unchanged JPG cover first. It is not embedded in the supplied deck.
2. Generate voice from the clean script plus the separate style directions. If the tool supports SSML, use the SSML version instead of the clean script for explicit pauses.
3. Put each title card during its corresponding silence. Do not speak the card title. Place the first after the opening personal story, the second before the fictional burden example and the third before the walkthrough.
4. Keep app navigation labels visible. Align each image to the corresponding passage in the separate timing guide. Speech should not say screen numbers or "next slide."
5. Add readable captions: one or two lines, high contrast, at least about 42–48 px on a 1920×1080 export. Put captions above the navigation label or in a separate safe area so the two never overlap. Allow for the video platform's controls and mobile crops.
6. Use one large highlight at a time. Suggested highlights: "$225 paid · 3 workdays · 8 caregiver hours"; "Review → correct → download"; "Your words → reviewed English summary"; "Barrier → possible action → follow-up." Clearly label the patient example fictional. Do not present Medicare spending as Lyme costs.
7. Listen once for meaning and once for timing. Keep the source limitations readable without covering the patient outputs. Test one small-phone playback.

The linked reference video could not be independently played in this environment. These edits implement the user's written feedback, not a claimed visual inspection of that video.
